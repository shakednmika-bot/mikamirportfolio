Bio

Berlin-based, self-taught multi-instrumentalist (guitar, bass, drums, sax, keys). She plays live for Georgia Cavallo and Shaked Miller, toured Germany on guitar with PORT NOO in 2026, including Bayerisches Fernsehen and radioeins, and joins Lauren Lakis on her European tour in April 2027. Before Berlin she played guitar and bass in Israel with Lea Haggai, Meirav Hellinger, Ori Kastiel, Avri G, MILLY and Yam Gronich, plus tribute shows for Aner Shapira and Mac Miller. As Mikamir she writes and self-produces Dark Groove (debut album Melting Ice Queen, 2025), and works freelance as a videographer and video editor for musicians.
